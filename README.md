# 🚀 ServiGo

### One Platform. Multiple Services. Smarter Experiences.

**ServiGo** is a unified digital platform designed to simplify how users discover, compare, and access services across **Retail, Hospitality, Entertainment, and Financial Services**.

The platform combines a clean and intuitive **UI/UX**, service discovery, price comparison, experience planning, personalized recommendations, and dedicated dashboards for **Users, Merchants, and Administrators**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 User / Customer

* Personalized user dashboard
* Product and service discovery
* Retail market price comparison
* Plan My Experience
* Personalized product recommendations
* Budget-based suggestions
* Product comparison assistance
* AI-powered basic platform/product assistance

### 🏪 Merchant

* Dedicated merchant dashboard
* Product and sales management
* Sales insights and trends
* Demand prediction
* Fast-moving and slow-moving product identification
* Low-stock and overstock alerts
* Inventory planning suggestions

### 🛡️ Admin

* Dedicated Admin Console
* User and merchant management
* Platform monitoring
* Service and product management
* Dashboard-based insights
